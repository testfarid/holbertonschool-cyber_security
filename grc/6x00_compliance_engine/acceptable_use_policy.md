# MedDefense Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy number:** SEC-AUP-001
**Effective date:** 2026-05-10
**Review date:** 2027-05-10 (Annual)
**Owner:** IT Security
**Approved by:** CISO

## Purpose
This policy protects MedDefense's information systems, networks, and patient data from unauthorized access, misuse, and security threats. It exists to establish clear, enforceable rules for using company technology resources while ensuring continuous compliance with healthcare regulations.

## Scope
This policy applies to every person with access to a MedDefense system. This strictly includes employees, contractors, vendors with remote access, and temporary clinical staff.

## Acceptable Use
MedDefense systems, network resources, and user accounts exist strictly for MedDefense business purposes and authorized clinical care delivery. Personal use of these systems is not permitted, and the concept of "reasonable personal use" is not recognized or authorized.

## Prohibited Activities
The following activities are strictly prohibited and non-negotiable:
* Accessing patient records outside the active care relationship for that patient.
* Sharing credentials or permitting another person to use your account.
* Installing unauthorized software on any MedDefense endpoint.
* Connecting personal devices to the clinical network without IT Security authorization.
* Transmitting MedDefense data using personal email, consumer cloud storage, or messaging applications.
* Attempting to access systems, data, or network segments beyond your assigned role.
* Entering or processing patient data into AI tools, external web services, or third-party platforms without an approved Business Associate Agreement.

## PHI Handling Obligations
Users must only access, use, or share the minimum amount of Protected Health Information (PHI) absolutely necessary to perform their specific clinical or administrative duties. Any handling of patient data beyond HIPAA minimum necessary requirements is a direct violation of this policy.

## Remote Access
Remote access to MedDefense systems is only permitted through authorized secure channels. Users are strictly prohibited from accessing MedDefense systems from public or shared Wi-Fi without an active VPN connection.

## Reporting Obligations
Users must report any suspicious email, anomalous system behavior, lost or stolen device, or accidental PHI disclosure. Reports must be made immediately upon discovery by submitting an IT Security ticket and calling the IT Security phone line.

## Consequences of Violation
Any violation of this policy will result in immediate action through the HR disciplinary process, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Additionally, violations may constitute HIPAA violations resulting in personal liability, including civil and criminal penalties.

## Acknowledgment and Review
All active personnel must review and sign this policy annually. Furthermore, new employees and contractors must sign this document at onboarding before system access is granted.
