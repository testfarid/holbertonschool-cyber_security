# MedDefense Acceptable Use Policy

**Policy number:** SEC-AUP-001
**Effective date:** 2026-05-10
**Review date:** 2027-05-10 (Annual)
**Owner:** IT Security
**Approved by:** CISO

## Purpose
This policy protects MedDefense computers, networks, and patient data from hackers, misuse, and theft. It gives you clear and simple rules for using company technology so we can keep our patients safe and follow the law.

## Scope
This policy applies to every person with access to a MedDefense system. This strictly includes employees, contractors, vendors with remote access, and temporary clinical staff.

## Acceptable use
MedDefense computers, internet, and accounts are only for MedDefense business and patient care. You are not allowed to use these systems for personal reasons. The phrase "reasonable personal use" is not an excuse.

## Prohibited activities
The following activities are strictly prohibited and non-negotiable:
* Accessing patient records outside the active care relationship for that patient (meaning you cannot look at records unless you are actively treating them).
* Sharing your passwords or letting anyone else use your account.
* Downloading or installing unauthorized software on any MedDefense endpoint without asking IT.
* Connecting your personal phones or laptops to the clinical network without IT permission.
* Sending MedDefense data using your personal email, consumer cloud storage, or private chat apps.
* Trying to access systems, data, or network segments beyond your assigned role.
* Putting patient data into AI tools, external web services, or third-party platforms without an approved Business Associate Agreement (a formal privacy contract signed by our company).

## PHI handling obligations
You must only view, use, or share the absolute minimum amount of patient data (Protected Health Information, or PHI) needed to do your specific job. Handling patient data beyond HIPAA minimum necessary requirements is a direct violation of this policy.

## Remote access
If you work from outside the hospital, you must use our approved secure tools. You are never allowed to access MedDefense systems from public or shared Wi-Fi (like at a cafe) without turning on the active VPN connection first.

## Reporting obligations
You must tell us if you see a suspicious email, anomalous system behavior, lose a device, or accidentally share patient data. You must report these events immediately upon discovery by submitting an IT Security ticket and calling the IT Security phone.

## Consequences of violation
If you break these rules, Human Resources will take disciplinary action, up to and including termination. Breaking these rules can also create HIPAA violations resulting in personal liability, meaning heavy fines or charges.

## Acknowledgment and review
All active personnel must review and sign this policy annually. Furthermore, new employees and contractors must sign this document at onboarding before system access is granted.
