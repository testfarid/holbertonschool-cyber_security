# Board of Directors Compliance Briefing

## Current compliance posture
Our recent comprehensive gap assessment reveals that while our technical defenses have significantly improved following recent incidents, our administrative governance is lagging, leaving MedDefense partially compliant but at risk during an OCR audit. We have established strong, measurable technical capabilities to protect patient data, but the lack of formally documented policies and vendor oversight currently prevents us from proving full HIPAA compliance to an external auditor. The situation is manageable, but requires immediate formal action.

## Top three findings before the audit
1. We cannot prove to an auditor that our workforce is bound by documented security rules, because the Acceptable Use Policy did not exist in writing until this week.
2. We lack formal oversight and legally binding agreements for third-party vendors handling patient data, exposing us to severe supply chain risks and regulatory fines.
3. We currently possess no documented security awareness training program, meaning we cannot demonstrate to regulators that our staff is prepared to recognize and stop threats like phishing.

## What we have completed
* Deployed centralized event logging and active threat monitoring rules across the environment.
* Implemented and documented strict endpoint security configuration baselines.
* Formalized and successfully tested comprehensive Incident Response playbooks and forensic runbooks.
* Drafted the missing core governance documents, including the Acceptable Use Policy, Data Classification Policy, and Vendor Management Policy.

## What must be completed before the audit
* Approve and distribute AUP, Data Classification Policy, and Vendor Management Policy (owner: CISO; deadline: one week before audit)
* Complete CloudVault Medical risk assessment and execute BAA (owner: IT Security and Legal; deadline: two weeks before audit)
* Document the security awareness training gap and provide an interim remediation plan to the auditor (owner: IT Security; deadline: before audit date)

## Board resolution requested
The board is requested to provide formal approval of the Acceptable Use Policy, Data Classification Policy, and Vendor Management Policy produced in this project.
